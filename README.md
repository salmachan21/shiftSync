# shiftSync

### Smart Shift Scheduling for Small Businesses

ShiftSync is a smart scheduling platform designed to simplify employee shift management for small businesses. It helps managers organize schedules, detect conflicts, identify uncovered shifts, and find suitable replacements when employees are absent.

## 🚩 Problem

Many small businesses manage employee schedules using spreadsheets and messaging applications. This can lead to:

- Scheduling conflicts
- Uncovered shifts
- Time-consuming absence management
- Inefficient communication between managers and employees

## 💡 Solution

ShiftSync centralizes employee scheduling into one platform.

The application provides three main functionalities:

- **Conflict Detection:** Automatically identifies overlapping shifts.
- **Uncovered Shift Detection:** Identifies shifts without assigned employees.
- **Replacement Suggestions:** Suggests available and qualified employees to replace absent workers.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. An employee reports an absence.
2. The system detects the uncovered shift.
3. Available and qualified employees are suggested.
4. The manager selects a replacement.
5. The schedule is automatically updated.

## 🎯 Target Market

ShiftSync targets small and medium-sized businesses, particularly:

Key risks include:

- Low customer adoption
- Competition from established scheduling platforms
- Employee data privacy
- Customer churn

Potential mitigation strategies include affordable local pricing, simple onboarding, multilingual interfaces, secure data management, and customer support.
